# REI Hub — Real Estate Investor Hub

**A complete underwriting and deal-execution toolkit for real-estate investors — in a single file. No install, no login, works offline.**

Open the app, and you're working immediately with a full set of sample deals. Add your own listings, leads, rehab jobs, and properties — everything you type is saved right in your browser, on your device.

## What you get — free

The free version organizes your entire deal workflow using numbers you enter yourself:

- **Dashboard** — command center with an interactive map, KPIs, price-drop alerts, and your hottest deal
- **Pipeline** — a lead CRM that moves deals from first contact to close, with a built-in FSBO checklist
- **Reno / Flip** — plan renovation timelines, budgets, and contractor rosters
- **Listings** — build an FSBO listing sheet and export it as a polished PDF
- **Calculators** — property-tax and transfer-tax estimators
- **Private & offline** — your data never leaves your device; the app keeps working with no connection
- **Light / dark theme**, one-click PDF export, and backup / restore

## Getting started

1. Open the live app link above (or open the app file in any modern browser).
2. Explore the sample deals that load automatically — or clear them and add your own.
3. That's it. Your work saves as you go.

Works in Chrome, Safari, Edge, and Firefox on desktop, tablet, or phone. Add it to your home screen for a one-tap, app-like launch.

## Your data & privacy

Everything you enter — properties, leads, budgets, notes — stays **on your own device**, saved in your browser. It's never uploaded and never shared. Keep a backup with the app's built-in **Export data** button before switching browsers or clearing history.
