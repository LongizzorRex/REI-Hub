Dashboard

Free

Your at-a-glance command center. Open it and you instantly see the health of your portfolio and which tracked listings need attention today. With Live Data the map and watchlist fill with real listings and prices.

Live KPI cards. Portfolio value, total equity, monthly cashflow, and average cap rate — recalculated from your real numbers every time you make a change.
GIS map with parcels & tax. Every owned and watched property is pinned on an interactive map. Click a marker to pull up its parcel APN, assessed value, zoning, and effective tax rate. Toggle between All, Owned, and Watch layers.
DOM tracker & price-drop alerts. Each tracked listing shows its days-on-market and flags any price cut versus the original ask — so motivated sellers surface themselves. A blinking alert strip calls out fresh drops and stale (90+ DOM) listings.
Top-signal deal card. The hub automatically ranks your watchlist and spotlights the single highest-scoring opportunity, with its cap, cash-on-cash, cashflow, and 70% max-allowable-offer — one tap to underwrite it.
Trailing-12-month cashflow chart. A rolling view of portfolio net cashflow so you can see the trend, not just the snapshot.

The deal workflow, end to end

Here's how the workspaces chain together on a single deal.

1
Spot it. Track a listing — it lands on the Dashboard map and watchlist with a Worth-Buying score and DOM/price-drop flags.
2
Underwrite it. Tap Analyze to open it in the Analyzer, tweak assumptions, and confirm the score and 70% offer.
3
Pursue it. Send the deal to the Pipeline as a lead and work it across the kanban using the FSBO checklist.
4
Renovate it. Once it's yours, build the rehab timeline, budget, and contractor roster in Reno / Flip.
5
Hold or sell it. Add it to the Portfolio to track cashflow and equity — or build a Listings sheet and sell.

Turning on Live Data

Live Data is a service we host and keep running for you. There are no API keys to get, no servers to set up, and nothing technical to configure — that's the whole point of the subscription. You paste one key and it works.

1
Subscribe to Live Data. After checkout you'll receive your license key by email (also shown on your receipt).
2
Click the Live Data button in the app header and paste the key into the box.
3
Click Activate. The status chip in the header switches to "Live Data on" and the first refresh runs immediately.
4
Leave it running. The app refreshes in the background on its own — check the "last updated" time in the header anytime.

What happens behind the scenes

Your app checks your key, then quietly requests the latest public figures (rates, listings, market stats) from our hosted service and drops them into the right screens. If the connection is slow or down, it simply keeps showing the last numbers it had — the app never freezes waiting on data.

Included with your subscription. Data refreshes, provider costs, and upkeep are all covered — you never manage or pay for any of it separately. Use your key on up to 3 devices; free a slot anytime with Live Data → Deactivate.

Back up & move your data

Since your deals live on your device, it's worth keeping a backup — and it's how you move your work to a new computer, browser, or phone.

The easy way

Use the built-in buttons in the app footer:

  • Export data — saves a backup file to your device.
  • Import data — loads that file on another device or browser.

Do this before switching browsers or clearing history.

Good habits

  • Export a fresh backup after a big work session.
  • Keep the backup file in your cloud drive (Dropbox, iCloud, Drive).
  • Use the same browser on the same device day-to-day so your data is always where you expect it.
Heads-up: your saved data is tied to the exact browser and device you use. Data in Chrome is separate from Safari; data in a normal window is separate from incognito. Pick one and stick with it, or use Export/Import to move between them.

Works offline & installs like an app

The status chip in the header reads "Live Data on" or "Saved local" when connected, and switches to "Offline mode" automatically if your connection drops — your data and every calculation keep working either way.

Add to home screen

iPhone/iPad: Share → "Add to Home Screen." Android: menu → "Add to Home screen." Desktop Chrome/Edge: the install icon in the address bar. It then opens full-screen like a native app.

What still works offline

Your saved deals, the pipeline, Reno/Flip, listings and the calculators. On Live Data, the Analyzer & Portfolio stay unlocked; only fresh live figures pause until you're back online — then they refresh on their own.

Billing & common questions

Do I need the paid version?

The free version organizes your deals — dashboard, pipeline, rehab budgets, listings and calculators. Live Data unlocks the Analyzer's Worth-Buying scoring and the Portfolio tracker, and fills in real rates, listings and market numbers automatically instead of by hand.

What happens if I cancel?

Live Data switches off and the app reverts to the free version — the Analyzer and Portfolio lock again. You keep all your saved deals and every free calculation; it just stops auto-refreshing.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes — click Live Data and paste your key on each device, up to 3 devices (e.g. phone, laptop, tablet). Free up a slot anytime by clicking Live Data → Deactivate on a device you no longer use. Move your saved deals between devices with Export/Import.

Is there anything to install or maintain?

No. The Live Data service is fully hosted and maintained by us. There are no keys to obtain, no servers to run, and no updates to install — you just use the app.

Where does the live data come from?

It's aggregated from trusted public and licensed real-estate data providers, refreshed on a schedule, and delivered to your app through our hosted service. Mortgage-rate data is sourced from FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.
